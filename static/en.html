More information

How it works

NexBot understands the question in natural language and answers from the information you give it: hours, services, prices, frequently asked questions. It remembers the last 6 exchanges of the conversation.

What you get

  • Natural answers, in your customer's language (French or English).
  • It only uses your information. If it doesn't know, it says so and invites the visitor to contact you, rather than making up a price, an hour or a policy.
  • You choose its tone and the topics it refuses to discuss.
  • If the AI service is unavailable, it answers from your FAQ instead of going silent.

Forwarding to your team

  • NexBot offers to forward the visitor's request, gets their agreement, then asks for their name and an email or phone number.
  • You receive an email with the summary, the priority (normal or urgent), the contact details, the language, the date and an excerpt of the conversation. If the visitor gave an email address, just reply to the message.
  • NexBot only confirms sending once the email has actually gone out. If it fails, it says so honestly and gives your contact details.

Good to know

  • The text of messages is sent to Google (Gemini model) only to generate the answers.
  • Like any virtual assistant, it can make mistakes. It does not replace your staff.
  • Abuse protection: 3 forwarded requests per conversation and 30 per day.
  • No commitment: cancel at any time.

FAQ

Your questions, answered.

How is NexBot installed on my website?

We set up your assistant, then you add a single line of code to your site. Nothing to install for your customers.

Are there any setup or installation fees?

No. Setting up and installing your assistant is free. You only pay the monthly subscription for your plan.

Which languages does NexBot answer in?

French and English. It replies in the language your customer uses.

Can NexBot make mistakes?

Yes, like any virtual assistant. It relies on your business's information, says when it doesn't know and invites your customer to contact you directly. It does not replace your staff.

What happens to my customers' conversations?

They are not saved in a database and are deleted automatically after the session. The only exception: if a visitor asks for their request to be passed on, they first agree, then an email is sent to your team. Details are in our privacy policy.

How does my team receive important requests?

By email, with a summary of the request, its priority (normal or urgent) and the visitor's contact details, only if the visitor agrees to be contacted. This feature is included in the Pro and Business plans.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes, with no penalty or minimum commitment. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period already paid. Details are in our terms of use.

Do prices include taxes?

No. Prices are in Canadian dollars, plus taxes, and the subscription is billed monthly.
